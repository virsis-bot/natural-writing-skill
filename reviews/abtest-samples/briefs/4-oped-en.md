# Task 4. Short opinion piece (EN)

Write a short opinion piece (500–800 words) for the blog of Halden Kitchen, an online kitchenware retailer, on the question: "Are smart kitchen appliances worth paying extra for?" Take a position. The byline is Dana Whitfield, head of product at Halden Kitchen, 9 years in the job. Only the article text (with a title), ready to publish.

Facts (do not add other facts, do not invent anything):
- At Halden Kitchen, the app-connected (Wi-Fi) version of an air fryer typically costs $40–60 more than the same model without Wi-Fi.
- In 2025 Halden surveyed 1,200 buyers of connected appliances after purchase. 61% had connected the app at least once. 18% were still using the app three months later.
- The most common thing people used the app for: a notification that cooking is done.
- Connected models were returned 1.4 times as often as their non-connected versions. The top return reason: "the app wouldn't pair."
- Exception: buyers of connected sous-vide cookers. 54% were still using the app after three months. Sous-vide cooks take hours, so being able to check and adjust from your phone is actually useful.
- Halden still sells both versions of most models.
